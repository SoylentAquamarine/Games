How to play

Goal: Retrieve the Enchanted Chalice (locked inside the Black Castle) and carry it back to the Gold Castle.

Move with the arrow keys / WASD or the on-screen pad. You can carry one item at a time — walk over an item to pick it up, press Space / “Drop / Use” to drop it.

Sword: touch a dragon while holding it to slay it. Without the sword, a dragon that catches you eats you (you respawn at the Gold Castle and drop what you held).

Black Key: touch the Black Castle gate holding it to open the castle. Bridge: carry it to walk straight through walls.

Three dragons roam: Yellow, Green, Red.
