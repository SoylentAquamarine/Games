❤❤❤🥚 0/12 · 🗝️ 0🪙 0

⚔️ Quest

Explore the dungeon, find the key, and beat the boss to claim its gemstone egg. Fill your carton with all 12 to unseal the final boss. Arrow keys/WASD to move, Space to swing your sword.

Move: arrows / WASD · Attack: Space · Roam the farmyard and find the burrow entrances hidden by barns, trees and haystacks. Walk into a farmhouse to look around (no enemies) — one of them is a general store where coins buy hearts and heart containers. Inside a dungeon, find the key and beat the boss for its gem egg — every direction from any room leads somewhere, and the 🚪 Leave button (top right, while inside) takes you back to the farmyard any time.
