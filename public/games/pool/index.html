🎱 Chicken Pool

Power
English
Tap the cue ball to strike off-centre — high for follow, low for draw, side for swerve. Double-tap to centre it.
Drag back from the cue ball and release to shoot.

Drag from the cue ball to aim — pull further back for more power, release to strike. 8-Ball: pot your group then the 8. 9-Ball: always hit the lowest ball first, sink the 9 to win. Billiards: no pockets — score by hitting both other balls in one shot. Mini: clear the small table in as few shots as you can.
