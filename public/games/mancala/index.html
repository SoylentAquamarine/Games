🫘 Mancala

Your turn — pick a pit on your row.

Sow stones counter-clockwise. Land your last stone in your store to go again; land in an empty pit on your side to capture the pit across from it.

How to play

Goal: collect the most stones in your store (the big pit on your right). The bottom row of 6 small pits is yours.

A turn: tap one of your pits to pick up all its stones and drop them one at a time into the following pits, going counter-clockwise. You drop into your own store but skip your opponent's store.

Go again: if your last stone lands in your own store, you take another turn.

Capture: if your last stone lands in an empty pit on your own side, you capture that stone and all the stones in the opponent's pit directly across from it — all into your store.

End: when one player's six pits are all empty, the other sweeps their remaining stones into their store. Most stones wins.
