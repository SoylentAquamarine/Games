Guess the 5-letter word.
How to play

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Type a word and press Enter. After each guess the tiles change colour:

Green — right letter, right spot.

Yellow — the letter is in the word, but a different spot.

Grey — the letter isn't in the word at all.

Use the clues to narrow it down. Tap the on-screen keys or use your keyboard (Backspace deletes).
