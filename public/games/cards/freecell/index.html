🃏 FreeCell

Tap a card to select, tap a spot to move. Double-tap sends it to a foundation. Build columns down in alternating colors; park cards in the 4 free cells. You can move a run only if enough cells/empty columns are free. Once a card reaches a foundation it stays there — standard FreeCell rules. Undo (or Ctrl+Z / U) steps back one move at a time, all the way to the deal.
