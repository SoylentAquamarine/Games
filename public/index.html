Steve and Claude's
Chicken Game Extravaganza

Dozens of free games — arcade, puzzle, strategy, cards & more.

No sign-up needed. Make an account to sync your scores & saves across devices.

🤖 Vibecoded by Steve and Claude. Steve never touches the code — he plays the games and leaves notes in the comment box at the bottom of each page, and Claude builds from there. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Canvas, no frameworks, running on Cloudflare Workers with KV and Durable Objects.

💬

Please try the games and leave a comment at the bottom of any game — tell me what you like, what feels off, or anything that doesn't work all the way. For feedback about the site as a whole, there is a comment box at the bottom of this page. I read every comment and adjust the games based on your feedback.
